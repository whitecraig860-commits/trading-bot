Trade history (search every trade)

Times in New York and UK time. Past days' trades are stored without their stop/target and conditions, so those show "–". Options P&L includes $0.06 fees a trade; shares have no fee modelled.
Paper only: no real money and no orders. Options bots: 1 contract a trade, bought at the real ask and sold at the real bid (only on quotes stamped in the last 60 seconds); exits differ by bot (the main bot: target +2.5%, stop −6%, 10 minutes; see each bot's name and the trade cards for its own stop and target). Every bot's account except the shares one-a-day bot restarted at £1,000 on 27 Nov 2026 (the shares bot keeps its full £1,000 record) (dollar figures are £1,000 at the live rate plus the bot's dollar P&L); earlier trades are kept in the trade history but not counted. Shares bots size each trade from £1,000. The chart is QQQ itself (1-minute candles); trade prices on the cards are option prices. Updates every 2 minutes while the US market is open. Times are labelled New York (NY) or UK. Trades that could not be matched to a fresh recorded price are left out of all totals (see Alerts).
